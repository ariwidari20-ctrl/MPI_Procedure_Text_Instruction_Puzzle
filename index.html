LEARN THE CONCEPT

What is a Procedure Text?

A procedure text explains how to make something or how to do something through clear, ordered instructions.

Social Function

To tell or explain how to make or do something.

Generic Structure

Goal → Ingredients / Materials → Steps

Language Features

Imperative verbs, action verbs, and sequence connectors.

Model Text: Making a Glass of Iced Tea

Goal
To make a glass of iced tea.
Ingredients
A tea bag, hot water, sugar, and ice cubes.
Steps
1. First, put a tea bag in a cup.
2. Next, pour hot water into the cup and let the tea brew.
3. Then, add sugar and stir until it dissolves. Let the tea cool.
4. Finally, add ice cubes and serve the iced tea.

Language clues

Imperative verbs: put, pour, add, stir, serve Sequence connectors: first, next, then, finally

Safety note: hot water should be handled by the teacher or an adult. In class, use a safe simulation if needed.

TEAM PROJECT

Instruction Puzzle: Rebuild the recipe

Work in a group. Select a sentence card from the mixed-up list, then choose an empty position on the right. On a touch screen, you can tap cards and slots. Use the buttons to move cards up or down.

Team roles: coordinator · reader · card arranger · language checker · presenter. Discuss before deciding.

MISSION 03

Present your project

Use the PID to display your final sequence. Each team member should contribute. Use this presentation guide:

  1. Introduce your recipe and explain its social function.
  2. Point out the Goal, Ingredients, and Steps.
  3. Explain why your team chose this order.
  4. Identify at least three imperative verbs and two sequence connectors.
  5. Demonstrate the instructions safely or use a simulation.
